# Howmet Aerospace (NYSE: HWM) — Equity Research

An initiating-coverage equity research report and supporting valuation model for Howmet Aerospace, a manufacturer of engineered metal components for jet engines, airframes, and industrial gas turbines.

**Rating:** BUY · **Price target:** $260.04 (1-year) · **Price at publication:** $201.77 (October 30, 2025) · **Implied return:** +28.9%

Published November 1, 2025. Analyst: Aditya Bakshi (Team 4).

## Summary

The thesis is that Howmet is a quality compounder rather than a value play. The recommendation rests on three points: structurally expanding margins (adjusted EBITDA margin moved from roughly 28% in 2024 toward 30% by Q3 2025), a leading position in the Engine Products value chain during an aftermarket-spares cycle, and a vertically integrated superalloy and titanium moat protected by long OEM qualification cycles.

Valuation is 100% DCF, blending a perpetuity-growth terminal value (35% weight, 3.5% terminal growth) with an exit-multiple terminal value (65% weight, 28.5x EV/EBITDA on 2030E), discounted at a 9.2% WACC. A set of engine-levered comparables carries 0% explicit weight and is used to corroborate the terminal multiple.

The workbook contains five tabs:

- **Cover Page.** Company, ticker, date, and share price inputs.
- **Operating Model.** Historical and projected income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow, from 2020 through the forecast period.
- **Discounted Cash Flow (DCF).** WACC build (4.0% risk-free rate, 1.2 beta, 4.5% equity risk premium, 3.4% after-tax cost of debt), unlevered free cash flow, and both terminal value approaches.
- **Comparable Companies (Comps).** Seven engine and aero specialty metals peers on CY2025E EV/EBITDA and P/E, against a peer median of 22.7x.
- **Valuation Exhibits.** Football field, a sensitivity grid of exit multiple against WACC, and bear/base/bull scenarios ($201 / $260 / $314).
