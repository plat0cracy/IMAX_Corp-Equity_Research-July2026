# IMAX Corporation (NYSE: IMAX): Equity Research

An initiating-coverage equity research report and supporting valuation model for IMAX Corporation, the premium large-format entertainment technology company that licenses its theatre systems and remasters films for its global network of roughly 1,800 commercial screens.

**Rating:** OVERWEIGHT · **Price target:** $45.00 (1-year) · **Price at publication:** $44.00 (July 23, 2026) · **Implied return:** +2.3%, with unpriced M&A and re-rating upside

Published July 24, 2026. Analyst: Aditya Bakshi.

## Summary

The thesis is that theatrical exhibition is bifurcating rather than recovering. As streaming absorbs casual viewing, the trips audiences still make to the cinema are increasingly premium events, and IMAX owns the format those events happen in. IMAX set a record for ticket sales in 2025 while the overall box office stayed about 30% below pre-pandemic levels.

The recommendation rests on three linked points: IMAX is an asset-light licensing and technology platform rather than an exhibitor, capturing roughly 17 to 18% of every IMAX box-office dollar at about 60% gross margin; premiumization and a proprietary-camera moat support an above-consensus 2026 box-office forecast of about $1.63B against management's $1.4B guidance; and those same forces justify a platform multiple rather than an exhibitor multiple, which is where most of the value is created.

Valuation is a 50/50 blend of a 10-year DCF ($35 per share) and trading comps ($56 per share, 14.5x IMAX's own 10-year median EV/EBITDA on 2026E). The DCF discounts equity free cash flow at a 10.7% cost of equity and blends an exit-multiple terminal value (65% weight, 12.5x EV/EBITDA on 2035E) with a perpetuity-growth terminal value (35% weight, 2.75% terminal growth). The IMAX China minority stake is deducted at market value. A takeover valuation ($51 per share, 35% control premium on a $38 unaffected price) is shown for reference at 0% weight, following the sale process reported in May 2026.

The workbook contains six tabs:

- **Assumptions.** Valuation inputs, historical anchors from the FY2025 10-K, key modelling decisions (network growth to 2,500 systems by 2035, box-office capture rate, film slate, FCF conversion path), and the CAPM cost of equity build (4.71% risk-free rate, 1.3 beta, 4.6% equity risk premium).
- **Model.** A 10-year operating model from 2026E to 2035E covering network size, IMAX box office per screen, revenue by stream, adjusted EBITDA, and equity free cash flow.
- **DCF.** Discounted equity free cash flow, both terminal value approaches, the non-controlling interest deduction, and a sensitivity grid of cost of equity against exit multiple.
- **Relative Val.** Implied value per share at 11x, 12.5x, and 14.5x EV/EBITDA on 2026E, 2027E, and street consensus EBITDA, set against exhibitor and platform multiple reference points.
- **Takeover.** Control-premium and takeout-multiple cross-checks on the reported sale process.
- **Synthesis.** Method weighting, the blended price target, and DCF-heavy, balanced, and comps-heavy weighting scenarios.

Bear / base / bull scenarios in the report are $33 / $45 / $58.
